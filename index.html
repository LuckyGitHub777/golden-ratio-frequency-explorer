Method

Nothing hidden.
Everything reproducible.

The experience is generated from explicit definitions. No frequency list is hand-picked to create a desired result.

01

Define φ

φ = (1 + √5) / 2

φ is the dimensionless irrational number approximately equal to 1.618033988749895.

02

Map ratio to frequency

fₙ = f₀ × φⁿ

Choose any positive anchor frequency f₀. Each integer step multiplies or divides it by φ.

03

Express the interval

c = 1200 log₂(r)

Cents express the logarithmic interval of frequency ratio r. A 2:1 octave equals 1200 cents by definition.

04

Fold into one octave

rₙ = 2((n log₂ φ) mod 1)

This preserves each φ-derived pitch class modulo octave equivalence. The 12 displayed source exponents are one finite sample of an irrational rotation.

05

Observe Fibonacci convergence

Fₙ₊₁ / Fₙ → φ

Ratios of consecutive Fibonacci numbers converge toward φ, alternating around it as n increases.

Evidence boundary

Keep the signal clean.

A compelling pattern is not automatically a physical mechanism. This package separates exact definitions and acoustic calculations from optional interpretation.

Established / defined

What the lab demonstrates

  • The exact mathematical definition and identities of φ.
  • Frequency ratios generated from integer powers of φ.
  • Period, cents, and approximate air wavelength calculated from explicit formulas.
  • Octave folding by powers of two.
  • Convergence of consecutive Fibonacci ratios toward φ.

Context / convention

What depends on a chosen reference

  • 440 Hz is the ISO 16 standard tuning frequency for the musical note A.
  • 432 Hz is offered here only as an alternate creative reference.
  • The 12-step octave-folded set is an experimental sample, not a universal tuning system.
  • 343 m/s is an approximate sound speed used only for the wavelength display.

Not established

Claims the package does not make

  • That 432 Hz or any φ-derived tone is biologically or cosmically privileged.
  • That these tones uniquely heal, alter DNA, or produce specific neurological effects.
  • That the geometry proves a paranormal or metaphysical mechanism.
  • That golden-ratio tuning is inherently superior to other tuning systems.

Symbolic, aesthetic, contemplative, spiritual, or metaphysical meaning may be explored as interpretation. It is not presented here as scientific proof.
